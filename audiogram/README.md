# Audiogram

Turn an audio clip — a podcast highlight, a voiceover, or a quote — into a short, captioned video that stops the scroll in a muted feed. A moving waveform or equalizer bars signal "there is sound here," captions carry the message for the 80% who watch on mute, and cover art, title, and a progress bar give it a finished, branded frame. For podcasters and creators repurposing audio into social video.

## When it activates

- "Make an audiogram" or "turn a podcast into a video."
- "Create a waveform video" or "add a moving waveform to my voiceover."
- "Convert audio to video" or "make a podcast clip video."
- "Make a square or vertical clip for a podcast quote."

## Example prompts

- "Make an audiogram from this podcast soundbite with synced captions, square for the feed."
- "Add a reactive equalizer and a progress bar to my voiceover, vertical for Reels."
- "Turn this 90-second quote into a captioned waveform clip with my cover art and title."

## What's inside

- `SKILL.md` — frame-driven waveforms (never a live analyser loop), bars vs. oscilloscope, the five-layer layout, captions synced to speech, 1:1 / 9:16 / 16:9 social framing, and an output checklist.
- `references/waveform-render.md` — complete runnable components for both looks, the full audiogram composition with cover art, title, progress bar and audio mux, a power-of-two/smoothing cheat sheet, and a dependency-free Web Audio + canvas variant.
